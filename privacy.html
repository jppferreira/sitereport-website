Privacy policy

Your job data stays on your device.

6 October 2026

Who Trade Report is for

Trade Report is a local-first app for tradespeople and small service businesses. It keeps a record of a finished job — photos, notes, and an optional customer signature — and turns that into a PDF report you can share.

What is stored, and where

Jobs, customer names, addresses, notes, photos, signatures, and generated PDFs are stored only in this app on your phone or tablet. The business details you add for the report — such as your name, logo, phone number, and email — stay there too.

Trade Report does not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes this information. Nothing about a job is uploaded automatically.

What is not collected

There is no account and no sign-in. There is no cloud database of customers or jobs, no advertising, and no analytics of customer or job information.

Network use is the in-app purchase flow that iOS or Android runs with the App Store or Google Play, and a bug report you choose to send from the app.

Trade Report does not use analytics or a crash-reporting service. A failure in the app is not sent anywhere unless you choose to write a bug report.

Bug reports

If you send a bug report from Settings, the text you write, the app version, and the device platform (such as iOS or Android) are sent to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). FormSubmit emails that message to the developer at hello@jppferreira.com. It does not receive job photos, signatures, customer details, or PDF reports. Sending a report is optional.

Purchases

The free tier includes 3 reports. Pro unlocks unlimited reports, branding, and customer signatures. The price is whatever the store shows when you buy.

Apple and Google process the payment under their own privacy policies. Trade Report receives confirmation that your purchase is active. It does not receive your card or payment details.

Sharing a PDF

A report stays on the device until you choose to share or save it. Sharing uses the phone’s share sheet. On Android you can also save a copy to a folder you pick. The file leaves the device only because you sent it.

From Settings → Data & privacy you can export a text copy of your job details and send that file yourself. Photo and signature files are not included in that export.

Deleting your data

You can delete a job from the job screen. That removes its photos, signature, and generated reports from the device.

You can delete all app data from Settings → Data & privacy. That clears jobs, photos, signatures, reports, and the business profile stored in the app. It does not cancel a purchase you already made with Apple or Google.

Children

Trade Report is a tool for people doing trade and service work. It is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@jppferreira.com

You can also reach the developer through the App Store or Google Play listing for Trade Report.
